# ORACLE LAUNCH VIDEO — Two 15s Clips (Alternating)

## Strategy
Two separate 15-second videos that alternate back and forth across the site and social posts.
- **Clip A — "The Hunt"** — Left guy, lost in the tape
- **Clip B — "The Know"** — Right guy, gets the alert, happy knowing smirk

The contrast IS the product. Never show both at once. Alternate between them.

---

## CLIP A: THE HUNT (15s)

**The Setup (0-3s):**
Dark room, 40s man leaning into two monitors. Red/green candlestick charts. No alert visible. His cursor hovers, then darts across the tape. Shoulders tight. Jaw clenched. He's hunting — frantically scrolling, squinting at the numbers. Red candles dominate. He doesn't know what's happening. He's chasing.

**The Frustration (3-7s):**
Still lost. Cursor moves faster now, scrolls in bursts. His shoulders get tighter. He scrolls back up. Types something. Deletes it. The red candles keep dropping. He leans forward, squinting. Not screaming — just the quiet tension of someone who knows something should be happening but can't see it.

**The Hold (7-12s):**
He's still hunting. One hand rests on the mouse, gripping it. The other rubs his jaw. The tape is a mess — red candles, no clarity. He refreshes. Waits. Refreshes again. No banner. No alert. Just him, alone with the chaos.

**The End (12-15s):**
Hold on his face — trapped in the hunt. Then fade to black.

---

## CLIP B: THE KNOW (15s)

**The Setup (0-3s):**
Same dark room, same kind of desk, different 40s man sitting back in his chair. Hands resting on the desk, relaxed. Two monitors show the same type of charts — calm, collected. He's not doing anything. Just waiting. Patient. His eyes are steady, not darting.

**The Banner (3-7s):**
A gold banner fades in over the right monitor's chart:
```
EVENT ALERT
WINDOW 14:00 · SENT 13:59:12
```
Matte gold (#e8b86d), serif font, subtle glow. No loud animation — just appears, like a clock turning over. The right man glances at it. A knowing smirk spreads across his face — not a wide grin, not victory, not a grin-to-camera. Just… quiet knowing. A slight smirk. The kind of smile someone gets when they already have the answer and everyone else is still looking.

**The Settle (7-12s):**
He's fully settled now. The knowing smirk is there, soft and confident. One hand lifts slightly to rest on his chin. The gold banner glows softly on his screen. He has it. The minute is in his hands. Everything else is noise. He doesn't need to scroll. He doesn't need to hunt. He already knows.

**The Lockup (12-15s):**
He smirks one more time. Confident. Calm. The banner holds. Then fade to black.

## Technical Specs (Both Clips)

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080)
- Duration: 15 seconds each
- Format: Cinematic, film grain optional
- Font: Serif (Cormorant Garamond style) for text overlays
- No voiceover
- No text on screen except the banner and end card in Clip B
- Banner text: "EVENT ALERT · WINDOW 14:00 · SENT 13:59:12"
- Color palette: #07080b dark, #e8b86d gold banner/text, #2ee58a green candles, #ff4d5a red candles
