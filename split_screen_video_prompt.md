# ORACLE LAUNCH VIDEO — 15s Cinematic Split Screen

## Deliverable
15-second 16:9 cinematic video, split down the middle with a thin gold vertical divider. Replace the single-grin hero on the site and the X post.

## Shot Breakdown

### 0–3s: THE SETUP
**Left panel:** Dark room, 40s man leaning into two monitors. Red/green candlestick charts, no alert. Cursor hovering, hunting. Shoulders tight, jaw clenched. The tape is chaotic — red candles dominate. He doesn't know what's happening.

**Right panel:** Same hour, same kind of dim desk, different 40s man sitting back in his chair. Hands resting on the desk, relaxed. Two monitors show the same type of charts but calm. No alert yet. He's waiting. Not doing anything.

**No banner visible yet on either screen.**

### 3–7s: THE BANNER APPEARS
**Left panel:** Still lost. Cursor moves faster, scrolls frantically. Shoulders tighter. The red candles keep dropping.

**Right panel:** A gold banner fades in over the right monitor's chart:

```
EVENT ALERT
WINDOW 14:00 · SENT 13:59:12
```

The banner is matte gold (#e8b86d), serif font, small text, subtle glow. No loud animation — just fades in like a clock turning over. The right man notices it with a single small glance. Not a wide smile. Not victory. Just… knowing. A slight knowing smirk. Minimal.

**Both charts stay mixed red/green.** The right chart does NOT turn green. The difference is the banner, not the candles.

### 7–12s: THE CONTRAST
**Left panel:** Still stuck. The man is scrolling, squinting, maybe typing. Frustrated. The tape is a mess — no clarity.

**Right panel:** He's settled. The knowing smirk is there. One hand lifts slightly to rest on his chin. The gold banner glows softly on his screen. He has it. The minute is in his hands. Everything else is noise.

### 12–15s: LOCKUP
**Both panels freeze.** Thin gold vertical divider between them.

**End card (2s overlay on both panels):**

```
WE KNOW THE WHEN.
YOU DO THE REST.
```

Gold text, serif, centered. Small URL: `amartiecom.com`. Then fade to black.

## Visual Rules

- **Color palette:** #07080b (dark), #e8b86d (gold banner/text), #2ee58a (green candles), #ff4d5a (red candles)
- **Gold divider** between left and right panels — thin, subtle
- **No P&L charts** (no dollar amounts, no profit numbers)
- **No BUY/SELL labels** on charts
- **No voiceover**
- **No "algo detection" text** or any framing about how it works
- **Right man does NOT turn into a green moon-shot chart.** Candles stay mixed.
- **Right man smirks** — not a grin-to-camera, not a victory laugh. Just quiet knowing.
- **Left man stays lost** — tense, hunting, no clarity

## Technical Specs

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1920×1080)
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Format:** Cinematic, film grain optional
- **Font:** Serif (Cormorant Garamond style) for text overlays
- **No voiceover**
- **No text on screen except the banner and end card**
- **The banner text must be readable:** "EVENT ALERT · WINDOW 14:00 · SENT 13:59:12"
- **End card text:** "WE KNOW THE WHEN. / YOU DO THE REST."
