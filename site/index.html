Verification method

Tape Witness — quiet matches, action diverges.

The ORACLE does not ask you to trust a screenshot. Tape Witness takes two clocks and lays them on the same minute grid: the published event windows, and the exact minutes you clicked at the broker. If the timing is real, the pattern is brutal and simple.

QUIET MUST MATCH

Quiet minutes match

Any minute with no broker click and no ORACLE window should be identical on both records: empty. If the call log is clean and you didn’t click, the tape is quiet. Silence is the control sample.

ACTION SHOULD DIVERGE

Action minutes diverge

A click is a divergence from the quiet tape. If you hedged the event, that click minute should sit inside the scheduled window — not three minutes early, not eighteen minutes late.

How you run it

  1. Export the session from your broker as CSV or blotter (timestamp, symbol, side). Minute resolution is enough — seconds are better.
  2. Load the same session’s ORACLE call log: asset, scheduled window, tier, published-before timestamp.
  3. Tape Witness buckets both files onto a one-minute grid in one timezone (exchange time, not local guesswork).
  4. Every minute is tagged QUIET or ACTION, then scored MATCH or OUTSIDE.
  5. You keep the file. The hash of the export plus the call log is the receipt — no one can rewrite the WHEN after you clicked.

Always-on session log

Trade Witness — the fill, not the click.

Tape Witness proves you clicked the WHEN. Trade Witness proves what actually filled. It stays on for the whole session, watches every fill, and flags anything that does not belong to a published window. It does not score direction. It does not score P&L. Those were never the product.

IN WINDOW

Fill inside the call

A fill whose timestamp sits inside a scheduled window — and follows a hedge click on Tape Witness — is a clean event fill. This is the only fill type that counts as “you traded the WHEN.”

ANOMALY

Fill that doesn’t belong

Off-clock fills, fills with no prior click, clicks with no fill, and fills that land long after the window closes are flagged. Noise is visible. The session cannot hide it.

What it watches

  1. Always on. Starts with the session. Does not wait for an alert. Every fill is logged whether an event is live or not — that is how off-clock noise becomes visible.
  2. Binds to Tape Witness. Each fill is paired to the nearest hedge click on the same symbol. No click → orphan fill. Click with no fill → ghost window.
  3. Windows only, not sides. Long or short is your mark. Trade Witness only asks: did the fill land inside the published minute?
  4. Lag. Seconds from click to fill are recorded. A 2-second fill in-window is clean. A fill that prints 11 minutes later is not the same trade as the click.
  5. Receipt. The running log is hash-chained. You can export it next to the Tape Witness file. Two clocks, one session, neither rewritten after the fact.

Anomaly book

IN WINDOW

Clean event fill

Click in window. Fill in window. Same symbol. Lag inside tolerance. No flag.

OFF CLOCK

Fill outside any window

You traded. The ORACLE had not called that minute. Counts as noise, not as an event.

ORPHAN

Fill, no hedge click

A fill appears with no Tape Witness action on that symbol. Manual, bot, or fat-finger — not an Oracle hedge.

GHOST

Click, no fill

You marked the window. Nothing filled. Rejected, unsent, or pulled. The WHEN was used; the book did not.

LATE

Fill after the window

Click was on time. Fill printed well after close. Slippage or queue — not the scheduled minute.

DOUBLE

Extra fills in one window

One hedge logged, more than one fill. Scale-in or accident. Flagged so the session cannot pretend it was a single event.
